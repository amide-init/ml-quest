# Code of conduct

ML Quest is a game for learning. Many people who arrive here are new to machine learning, to open source, or to both, so we keep this a place where it's safe to not know things yet.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. There are no stupid questions here.
- Critique ideas and code, never people. Say what would make it better.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Credit other people's work, including level ideas and playtesting.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity, background or ability.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.

## Where it applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, [@amide-init](https://github.com/amide-init), through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may block people who break this code.

This code draws on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
